One short, funny rant (388 characters) about an everyday annoyance: the
self-checkout machine that accuses you of stealing in front of a queue
of strangers.

> The self-checkout machine just accused me of stealing in front of a queue of strangers. "UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA." It was my bag. I put it there. I scanned the avocado, I paid for the avocado, and still this beige robot treats me like a smuggler. Now I apologise to a machine for buying groceries. It beeps. I flinch. We both know who is in charge, and it is not the customer.

`main.py` is stdlib-only and deterministically copies `entry.txt` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, validating it is 1–400 characters and
verifying the copy by SHA-256.
